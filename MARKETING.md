## Discord/论坛

### Obsidian 社区发帖
```
# [Plugin] Tab Settings Enhanced v1.0.0 - Smart Tab Management

Hi everyone! 👋

I've just released **Tab Settings Enhanced**, a plugin to make tab management in Obsidian more intuitive.

## Features
- **New Tab Placement**: Control where new tabs open (after parent, before parent, first, last, after pinned)
- **Close Activation**: Choose which tab becomes active after closing (parent, left, right, most recent, first, last)
- **Parent-Child Colors**: Visual color coding for related tabs
- **Duplicate Prevention**: Switch to existing tab instead of opening new
- **Bilingual**: Full EN/ZH support

## Why I made this
When researching, I open many links from a single note. Without this plugin, they scatter randomly and I lose context. Now they stay grouped and I can easily navigate back.

## Install
Search "Tab Settings Enhanced" in Community Plugins, or:
GitHub: https://github.com/wonderez/tab-settings-enhanced

Feedback welcome! 🙏
```
